# og_cancer_risk
This repository shares the R and SQL code for our analysis of clinical features present in the electronic health records of English primary care patients between 2007 - 2018, using CPRD Aurum data. The results of our analysis are pending imminent publication in BJGP as Risk of oesophago-gastric cancer in English primary care patients with a symptom or symptomatically-similar disease diagnosis (doi pending).

Please note - the scripts encompass additional analyses within my PhD thesis chapter for my studentship with Health Data Research UK as part of their Big Data for Complex Disease programme. Hence not all pre-processing, analysis and outputs included within the scripts are relevant to the publication.
